About the System

This system provides immutable, transparent tracking of goods across multi-tier supply chain networks. By leveraging smart contract state machine governance, raw material suppliers, manufacturers, regional distributors, retailers, and end consumers collaborate seamlessly while maintaining absolute data integrity.

In the event of supply chain delays, capacity breaches, or operational bottlenecks, the integrated Disruption Engine automatically detects anomalies, recalculates projected delivery dates, and ranks candidate replacement participants using multi-factor scoring algorithms.

Solidity Smart Contracts

All user role approvals, raw material specs, finished products, order milestones, and leg status transitions are recorded immutably on the Ethereum blockchain.

Automated Disruption Engine

Rule-based background scanner evaluates delivery deadlines, flags delayed shipment legs, calculates downstream impact, and ranks alternative replacement candidates.

AES-GCM & IPFS Encryption

Sensitive documents (quality certificates, delivery invoices) are encrypted client/server side using 256-bit AES GCM before pinning to IPFS/Pinata.

End-to-End Traceability

Complete 4-leg shipment timeline tracking, providing full audit history, verified participant addresses, and real-time order status updates.

Multi-Role Capabilities

System Admin Approve/reject participant registrations, inspect system audit logs, and view real-time network analytics.
Raw Material Supplier Register raw materials, declare production capacity, and dispatch Leg 1 shipments to manufacturers.
Manufacturer Register finished product catalog, assign suppliers/distributors, and dispatch Leg 2 shipments.
Regional Distributor Assign regional retail outlets and dispatch Leg 3 shipments to retail stores.
Retail Store Receive inventory and execute final Leg 4 last-mile consumer delivery completion.
Consumer Browse product catalog, place orders, and trace complete end-to-end shipment progress timeline.
