STEP 2.3: CONSUMER PLACES AN ORDER
  1. Log in at /login with Role: "Consumer", Username: "JohnConsumer", Password: "consumer123"

  3. Enter:

  2. Under "Orders & Leg 2 Fulfillment", find your new Order ID.

     - Supplier: ApexMaterials
     - Distributor: ExpressDistributors (or your preferred distributor)

  5. Click "Logout".

STEP 2.5: SUPPLIER DISPATCHES LEG 1 (Supplier -> Manufacturer)
  1. Log in as Supplier ("ApexMaterials" / "supplier123").
  2. Under "Leg 1 Shipments", find the order and click "Create Leg 1".
  3. Change status from "PREPARING" -> "SHIPPED" -> "DELIVERED".
  4. Click "Update Shipment Status".
  5. Leg 1 is now recorded on-chain as DELIVERED.
  6. Click "Logout".

STEP 2.6: MANUFACTURER DISPATCHES LEG 2 (Manufacturer -> Distributor)
  1. Log in as Manufacturer ("OmniMfgCorp" / "mfg123").
  2. Under "Leg 2 Fulfillment", find the order and click "Create Leg 2".
  3. Change status to "DELIVERED" and click "Update Shipment Status".
  4. Click "Logout".

STEP 2.7: DISTRIBUTOR ASSIGNS RETAILER & DISPATCHES LEG 3 (Distributor -> Retailer)
  1. Log in as Distributor ("ExpressDistributors" / "dist123").
  2. Under "Incoming & Outgoing Shipments", find the order.
  3. Assign Retailer: Select "MetroRetailStore" and click "Assign Retailer".
  4. Click "Create Leg 3".
  5. IMPORTANT: Change status from "IN_TRANSIT" to "DELIVERED" and click "Update Shipment Status".
     * WARNING: If you leave Leg 3 in "IN_TRANSIT", the final order cannot be completed!
  6. Click "Logout".

STEP 2.8: RETAILER DISPATCHES LEG 4 (Retailer -> Consumer)
  1. Log in as Retailer ("MetroRetailStore" / "retailer123").
  2. Under "Leg 4 Last-Mile Delivery", find the order and click "Create Leg 4".
  3. Change status to "DELIVERED" and click "Update Shipment Status".
  4. Full order delivery is now complete!

SECTION 3: DISRUPTION SIMULATION & RECOVERY WORKFLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What is a Disruption?
A disruption occurs when a shipment leg is delayed, carrier transit fails, or a participant
breaches capacity/lead times.

STEP 3.1: HOW A DISRUPTION IS TRIGGERED
  There are two ways a disruption appears in the system:
  Option A (Automatic Scanner):
    - Go to /disruptions and click the blue "Run Disruption Scanner" button.
    - The engine inspects expected vs. actual delivery dates across all active legs.
  Option B (Manual Flagging):
    - When updating any leg status in Supplier, Manufacturer, Distributor, or Retailer
      dashboards, select "DELAYED" or "DISRUPTED" in the status dropdown and submit.

STEP 3.2: VIEWING THE DISRUPTION IN THE DISRUPTION CENTER
  1. Navigate to http://127.0.0.1:5000/disruptions (or click "Disruptions" in the top navbar).
  2. You will see a table titled "Recorded Supply Chain Disruptions".
  3. Locate your order (e.g. ORD-1790665550) with Disruption Type "TRANSPORT_DELAY".
  4. Under the "ACTIONS / RECOMMENDATIONS" column, click the blue button:
     "View Recommendations".

STEP 3.3: UNDERSTANDING THE RECOMMENDATIONS SCREEN
  The screen will display:
  - Downstream Impact Analysis: Shows the calculated delay (e.g., 4 days) and projected new delivery date.
  - Target Role to Replace: E.g., "DISTRIBUTOR" or "SUPPLIER".
  - Candidate Ranking Table:
    * Candidate Username (e.g., ExpressDistributors, dis1)
    * Eth Address
    * Capacity & Lead Time
    * Past Disruptions count
    * Rank Score (e.g., 105.0 - HIGHLY_RECOMMENDED)

STEP 3.4: HOW TO RESOLVE & SWITCH PARTICIPANT ON-CHAIN
  1. In the Candidate table, locate the top-scoring candidate.
  2. Look at the "ACTION" column:
     - You will see the green button:
       [ Confirm & Switch on Chain ]
  3. Click "Confirm & Switch on Chain".
  4. What happens automatically:
     - The smart contract executes `updateOrderDistributor` / `updateOrderSupplier` / `updateOrderRetailer`.
     - An immutable `RoleSwitched` event is recorded on Ethereum Ganache.
     - An automated email notification is sent to the new participant.
     - A green flash message confirms: "Successfully confirmed replacement on Blockchain!".

STEP 3.5: COMPLETING THE RECOVERED SHIPMENT
  1. Log in as the newly assigned candidate account.
  2. The reassigned order will now appear on their dashboard.
  3. Proceed with the shipment leg and mark status as "DELIVERED".
  4. The bottleneck is cleared, and downstream legs can proceed smoothly!

SECTION 4: VERIFYING TRACEABILITY & AUDIT TIMELINE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4.1: OPEN TRACEABILITY PAGE
  1. Click "Traceability" in the top navigation bar (http://127.0.0.1:5000/traceability).
  2. The page automatically loads the most recently updated order.
  3. Alternatively, use the "Quick Select Order" dropdown to select any Order ID.

STEP 4.2: AUDITING THE TIMELINE
  Inspect the visual progress stepper:
  - Consumer (Cart Icon) -> Shows consumer ETH address & order creation.
  - Step 1 (Supplier)    -> Leg 1 status (Green = DELIVERED).
  - Step 2 (Mfg)         -> Leg 2 status (Green = DELIVERED).
  - Step 3 (Distributor) -> Leg 3 status (Green = DELIVERED, Blue = IN_TRANSIT).
  - Step 4 (Retailer)    -> Leg 4 status (Green = DELIVERED).
  - Overall Order Status Badge -> Shows "DELIVERED" once all legs are complete.

STEP 4.3: INSPECTING SHIPMENT LEG AUDIT HISTORY
  Below the timeline, review the "Shipment Leg Audit History" table:
  - Leg IDs (e.g. LEG1-ORD-..., LEG2-ORD-...)
  - Sender & Recipient Ethereum addresses (clickable / verifiable)
  - Status badges
  - Expected Dates & actual timestamps
  - IPFS Document Hashes (e.g. QmLeg1Doc, QmLeg2Doc)

========================================================================================
                             TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON MISTAKES
========================================================================================
Q: Leg 4 shows DELIVERED, but the order status is still "SHIPPED". Why?
A: Leg 3 (Distributor -> Retailer) was left as "IN_TRANSIT". Log into the Distributor
   account and mark Leg 3 as "DELIVERED".

Q: In Disruptions, I see a yellow notice instead of the Confirm button. Why?
A: The template has now been updated to show the "Confirm & Switch on Chain" button to
   all logged-in participants (Retailer, Manufacturer, Distributor, Supplier, Admin).
   Simply refresh your browser page (F5) to see the green button.

Q: How do I test with new accounts?
A: Register at /register with any unique username. Then log in as Admin (/admin_login)
   and click "Approve" next to the new user.
